Features

Everything your team needs

From a solo learner to a full school robotics program — Vexify has the tools to keep everyone on track.

Structured Curriculum
30 expert-written lessons across Foundations, Design & CAD, and Programming tracks. Read, learn, mark complete — earn VCoins along the way.
Class Calendar
Teachers schedule and assign lessons by date. Students see exactly what's coming, what's past due, and what they've already completed.
Real-time Messaging
Every class gets its own live chat channel powered by Firebase. Ask questions, share updates, and keep everyone in sync.
Help Ticket System
Students submit support requests directly in the app. Teachers track, prioritize, and resolve tickets — no separate tool needed.
VCoins & Reward Shop
Students earn VCoins for every lesson they complete. Teachers can award bonus coins too. Spend them in the shop on badges and digital rewards.
Progress Tracking
Teachers get a bird's-eye view of every student: lessons completed, VCoins earned, grades logged. No spreadsheet required.
Class & Roster Management
Create multiple classes, generate join codes, add members, rename classes, and manage your whole roster from one panel.
Admin Dashboard
School admins control the whole platform — invite teachers, manage users, view platform-wide stats, and configure settings.
